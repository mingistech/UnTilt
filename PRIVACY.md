# UnTilt Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

## Mac App Store version

UnTilt processes photos locally on your Mac. The app does not collect, upload, transmit, or share your photos, file names, or personal information with the developer or third parties.

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The Mac App Store version does not make network requests.

## Photo and folder access

UnTilt accesses photos you explicitly open or drop into its window. It may ask you to select the original folder so it can save an edited copy beside the original. The original photo is not overwritten. Your files remain on your Mac or in the storage location you selected.

## GitHub version

The version distributed through GitHub additionally offers manual and periodic update checks. Those checks contact GitHub to request the latest public release information. GitHub receives ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy. Photos and photo contents are never included in update checks. The date of the last successful check is stored locally.

## Support

If you choose to contact us through a GitHub issue, the information you submit is handled by GitHub and may be public. Do not include private photos or other sensitive information in public issues.

Questions about this policy can be raised at https://github.com/mingistech/UnTilt/issues.

## Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app. The effective date above identifies the current version.
